# OmaCap 0.1.1

- Recording a still window no longer stops after 12 seconds without a screen update. Still frames also retain their timing through the end of the recording.
- Editor playback no longer repeatedly seeks audio and camera tracks during normal playback, avoiding audio skips and uneven camera playback. Seeking and cuts still align the tracks.
- Interrupted recordings with missing duration metadata can be recovered without changing the original media.
