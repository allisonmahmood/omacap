# OmaCap 0.1.2

- Crop opens a dedicated view of the full recording, even when the existing crop is tight. Drag the selection corners to resize it or drag inside to move it.
- Confirm crop applies the selection to the styled preview in one undo step. Cancel keeps the previous crop; Reset selects the full recording without applying it until confirmation.
- Includes the recording, playback, and interrupted-session recovery fixes from 0.1.1.
