OmaCap 0.2.1 fixes recordings that stopped as soon as they started on machines with an NVIDIA encoder.

## Changes

- Recordings no longer end at once with "OmaCap couldn't check the display, so it stopped recording." This happened when recording with NVIDIA's encoder after GStreamer was updated to 1.28.9: the encoder now restamps its frames, and OmaCap read those stamps to check that the display hadn't changed. It now works out when a frame was captured without them. Recordings made with the software encoder were not affected.

For everything new in 0.2.0, including screenshots and the camera's background blur, see the [0.2.0 notes](https://github.com/allisonmahmood/omacap/releases/tag/v0.2.0). They also cover updating from 0.1.3.

## Install or update

Download `omacap-0.2.1-1-x86_64.pkg.tar.zst` and `SHA256SUMS` from the assets below. In the download directory, run:

```sh
sha256sum --check --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS
sudo pacman -U ./omacap-0.2.1-1-x86_64.pkg.tar.zst
```

Finish or export your current edit before updating. GitHub releases do not update through `yay` or the Omarchy updater.

If you previously used the per-user installer, `~/.local/bin/omacap` and its desktop entry take precedence over the package. See the README's installation section when switching installation methods.

## Validation and limits

Built against Omarchy stable, with Omasnap's smoke tests, the integration tests and a fresh-container installation and launch check. The PR also passed on current Arch. The fix was checked by recording through a real screen-cast portal in a nested Hyprland with NVIDIA's encoder on GStreamer 1.28.9, where 0.2.0 failed every time. The automated tests have no NVIDIA encoder, so they check the timing rule on its own.

The limits of 0.2.0 still apply. Rotated monitors can't be recorded yet, a display change during a recording ends it, and exports still need Xwayland. Real microphone and camera behaviour depends on your devices, so check a short recording first.
